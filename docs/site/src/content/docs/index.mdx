<Aside type="tip" title="v0.5.0 - Formulas know their parents">
The block of formulas is complete: thirty formulas that open once the ones they stand on are sewn, said by typing and checked turn by turn - a slipped shape, a missed word and a wrong order told apart and mended apart - with words learnt inside their sentences, every sentence heard, and nouns and verbs of your own in the drill. The reader and the compiler are what the next releases build.
</Aside>

## What hilvan is

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Formulas first" icon="document">
		Grammar is drilled as formulas by substitution - the same pattern, different words - so production practice starts on structures you can already half-manage.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Repetition that measures" icon="star">
		Spaced repetition (FSRS) decides what to review and when: every formula is new, basted or sewn, and reviews always come before anything new.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Material compiled for you" icon="rocket">
		Formulas today; an LLM compiling sentences and audio lessons from your own learner model comes next - a compiler of material, not a chatbot.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
