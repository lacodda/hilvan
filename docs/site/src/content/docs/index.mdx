## What hilvan is

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Formulas first" icon="document">
		Grammar is drilled as formulas by substitution - the same pattern, different words - so production practice starts on structures you can already half-manage.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Repetition that measures" icon="star">
		Spaced repetition (FSRS) decides what to review and when, and a reader measures how much of a real text you already know - coverage as the honest progress metric.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Material compiled for you" icon="rocket">
		An LLM compiles sentences and audio lessons from your own learner model - known formulas and words - and a TTS provider voices them. A compiler, not a chatbot.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
