# The English lexicon

`lexicon.tsv` is the list of English words hilvan knows about: ten thousand
lemmas, commonest first, each with its inflected forms and a transcription.
It is what places a word in a level - the commonest 1000, 2000 or 5000 - what
tells the pack loader that *went* is a form of *go*, and what the word card
shows under the word.

It is built into the binary rather than loaded, so the version of hilvan is
the version of its lexicon.

```
rank <TAB> lemma <TAB> IPA (American) <TAB> other forms, space-separated
42	go	ɡoʊ	goes going gone gos went
```

The file is generated by [`tools/lexicon/build.py`](../../tools/lexicon/build.py)
and is not edited by hand: change the builder and run it again.

## What was changed

The sources are not copied: they are combined. Spellings are folded into
lemmas by the database's forms; a spelling two lemmas share is split between
them by how the treebank tagged it, or by how often each lemma is met in
spellings nobody else claims; a spelling that is mostly a name counts for the
share of the time it is not; British spellings, letters, abbreviations and
names are left out; lemmas are ranked by the frequency that results, and the
first ten thousand kept. Transcriptions are the dictionary's first
pronunciation, rewritten in IPA with the stress marks before their syllables.

## Known artefacts

wordfreq's sources run to 2021 and the treebank is older, so a spelling that
was a name only lately - *trump* - keeps the frequency of the name. These
sit well past the levels a pack teaches from, and are left as they are rather
than patched one by one.
